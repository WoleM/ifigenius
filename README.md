# Important Announcement Regarding Repository Data

It has come to my attention that certain third-party private companies have been utilizing the `ai_bot.data` file for their own personal gain, without any acknowledgment or credit for the work that has gone into its creation. This blatant disregard for the effort and dedication invested in this project has compelled me to take decisive action.

As a result, I have made the difficult decision to halt and remove the machine learning data from this repository. However, I believe in the spirit of sharing knowledge and resources, and thus, I will retain some of the report files detailing the 25 highest experience mobs per 10 level. These can be found in the following directory:

- [EXP mobs for IL](IL/exp_files)

To those who are new to this repo, I encourage you to explore the contents of the `IL/exp_files/` folder. Additionally, I may include information on the highest money-making spots if there is sufficient interest.
